No heat? Check these first

Five quick checks before you call.

Some no-heat calls come down to a dead thermostat battery or a switch someone flipped. Walk through these and you will either fix it or know exactly what to tell Keith.

Smell gas? Do not check anything. Leave the house now and call your gas utility or 911 from outside.

General guidance only, not professional advice for your system. Every boiler is different. If anything looks wrong or you are unsure, stop and call. Sample feature in this concept.

Check 1 of 5

Thermostat

  • Is it set to Heat, not Cool or Off?
  • Is the set temperature a few degrees above the room?
  • Blank screen? Try fresh batteries.

Check 2 of 5

Breaker and boiler switch

  • Look for a tripped breaker labeled boiler or burner and reset it once.
  • Find the boiler's service switch, often red, near the stairs or boiler. Make sure it is on.
  • If the breaker trips again, leave it off and call.

Check 3 of 5

Pilot or ignition

  • If your boiler has a viewing window, is there a flame or a pilot light?
  • Some boilers have a reset button. Press it once only. Repeated resets can flood the burner.
  • Only relight a pilot if the label on your boiler gives instructions and you are comfortable. Otherwise, call.

Check 4 of 5

Pressure gauge

  • Hot water boilers have a gauge on the front or on the pipes.
  • Note the reading. Many home systems sit somewhere around 12 to 15 psi when cool, but check your boiler's label.
  • Very low, or close to 30 psi? Do not adjust it yourself. Call and tell Keith the number.

Check 5 of 5

Water level (steam boilers)

  • Steam boilers have a small glass tube on the side showing the water level.
  • It is often around halfway. Empty or full to the top means something needs attention.
  • Never add water to a hot boiler that has run dry. Turn it off and call.

All five checked

Time to call Keith.

You have ruled out the easy stuff, and that helps. When you call, mention what you saw: the thermostat setting, the pressure reading, and whether there was a flame.
